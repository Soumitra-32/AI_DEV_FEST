# Shonchoy Copilot: Track 03 Final Plan
AI DEV FEST 2026 | DIU CPC x upay | Track 03: Customer Innovation & Financial Independence

---

## 1. One-liner
Bangla-first AI coach that turns confusing upay transactions into plain-language guidance, a realistic savings plan, and a 14-day cash-flow forecast, without upselling anything.

## 2. Problem Statement (official template)
For cash-dependent, irregular-income upay users, month-end cash shortfalls and repeated avoidable cash-out fees cause missed savings goals, borrowing stress, and lost money every month. We will build Shonchoy Copilot, a Bangla-first AI financial coach that uses transaction history to forecast cash flow, detect avoidable spending, and generate a feasible savings plan, with success measured by (a) taka saved in avoidable fees, (b) shortfall days avoided, (c) savings-goal hit-rate, and (d) forecast MAE vs baseline.

## 3. Idea Development Framework (9-step logic chain)

| # | Step | Our answer |
|---|------|------------|
| 1 | User | Cash-dependent wallet user with irregular income (day laborer, small shopkeeper, gig worker, student with family remittance). Persona "Rahim". |
| 2 | Problem | Runs short before month-end, makes many small cash-outs that carry fees, cannot read transaction history, and has no realistic way to plan a goal. Baseline: simulated avg cash-out share, avg fees paid/month, avg shortfall days/month (measured on the generated dataset). |
| 3 | Why now | Wallet transaction history is rich but unused for coaching. LLMs can now explain in Bangla, and light ML (LightGBM, IsolationForest) runs cheaply. |
| 4 | Solution | Next.js Bangla/English app with voice. Flow: transactions explained -> health score -> forecast -> savings plan -> fee-switch suggestion -> tips -> consistency signal. |
| 5 | AI role | Forecasting (LightGBM), anomaly detection (IsolationForest), classification and scoring (LogisticRegression), optimization (savings feasibility solver), generation (LLM Bangla explanation), retrieval (RAG tips). |
| 6 | Impact | Fees saved (BDT/month), shortfall days avoided, goal hit-rate, % of users with a feasible plan. |
| 7 | Data | Fully synthetic: 500 users x 6 months (Faker/Pandas). No real data or PII. |
| 8 | Validation | Offline: MAE vs baselines, anomaly precision/recall vs fixed rule, AUC on a held-out set. Simulation: backtest the plan (plan on months 1-4, test on months 5-6). |
| 9 | Scale | Same API contracts accept real upay data. See Section 14. |

## 4. Why AI and not just rules (product-readiness check)

| Component | Simple rule baseline | AI approach | Evidence we will show |
|-----------|----------------------|-------------|-----------------------|
| Cash-flow forecast | 7-day seasonal naive (same weekday last week) and trailing average | LightGBM with calendar, lag, rolling, and persona features | MAE/RMSE comparison on held-out users, target 15% or more improvement over best baseline |
| Anomalies | Fixed threshold (amount > X) | IsolationForest on per-user behavior features | Precision/recall on injected anomalies. Rule misses user-relative anomalies (a small amount can be abnormal for a low-spend user) |
| Credit readiness signal | Hand-weighted score | LogisticRegression with SHAP | AUC on held-out set plus top-3 reasons per user |
| Savings plan | Goal / months | Constraint solver on the forecasted surplus (not just average income) | Show where the naive plan fails during pressure days and the solver's plan survives |
| Tips | Static list | Behavior-triggered RAG | Same user gets different tips based on their flags |

Rule: math and decisions live in Python rules or models. The LLM only verbalizes structured JSON and never invents numbers.

## 5. Features and Opportunity Coverage

1. **Health Coach:** score 0-100 from cash dependency %, savings rate, fee burden, and balance volatility. The LLM writes a Bangla summary from the structured metrics.
2. **Savings Planner:** goal + months -> feasible monthly amount from forecasted surplus -> trade-off options (extend time, lower target, cut a category) -> do-nothing option.
3. **Spending Companion:** IsolationForest anomalies plus avoidable-fee detection, with a suggested reduce/delay/switch action.
4. **Cash-Flow Forecasting:** LightGBM 14-day inflow/outflow, pressure days (typically 28-31), explained by top drivers.
5. **Goal Copilot:** templates for education, emergency fund, travel, device, family.
6. **Inclusive Assistant (core):** Bangla/English toggle, voice input (Web Speech API, bn-BD), large simple UI, suggestion chips as a text fallback.
7. **Literacy Personalizer:** RAG over 30 curated tips, triggered by behavior flags (for example "frequent small cash-outs" -> fee tip). Each tip shows why it was chosen.
8. **Financial Consistency Signal** (renamed from credit score): see the next section.

### Financial Consistency Signal (replaces "credit score")
- Not a credit score and not a lending decision. Educational only.
- Output: a band (Building / Steady / Strong) with top-3 SHAP factors and "what could improve it" in plain Bangla.
- Banner on every view: "This is not a loan eligibility decision. upay and lenders make no automatic decision from this."
- No "+15 pts" language. Use "moving from Building toward Steady if you do X."
- Label creation (anti-circularity): the synthetic outcome ("kept stable balance and met obligations next 2 months") is generated from a latent stability variable plus noise and random shocks. It is not computed from the same formula as the features. Report AUC on held-out users, and state that this is a demo of the method, not a validated risk model.

## 6. Empowerment Guardrails
- No upsell, no loan push, no "buy now". Suggestions are reduce, delay, or switch only.
- Every card shows **Prediction / Assumption / Explanation** separately, plus a **"Do nothing"** option and the cost of doing nothing.
- Human confirmation is required for any action. Nothing is autonomous, and there is no approve/deny anywhere.
- Banned-output filter: blocks loan offers, urgency language ("hurry", "limited time"), guaranteed-return claims, and product promotion.
- Security:
  - Prompt-injection defense: the LLM receives only structured JSON context, never raw user text as instructions. User text is classified into whitelisted intents, and system and user prompts are separated.
  - Access control: user_id comes from the demo auth token, not the request body, so one user cannot query another.
  - Input validation (Pydantic), rate limiting, and logs with no PII.
  - LLM fallback: if the API fails, use template-based Bangla explanations so the demo never breaks.
- Fairness: report metric gaps across persona, district, and income band (see Section 10).

## 7. Synthetic Data Strategy (DATA_ASSUMPTIONS.md content)

**Scale:** 500 users x 6 months, SQLite.

**Schema (transactions):** user_id, persona, district, income_band, timestamp, type, channel, category, amount_bdt, fee_bdt, balance_after.
**Users table:** user_id, persona, district, income_band, age_band, language_pref.

**Personas (document each):**
- Daily wage earner (irregular daily income)
- Small shopkeeper (merchant-heavy inflows, weekend peaks)
- Salaried worker (income at month start or end)
- Student (family remittance, low spend)
- Gig/rider (weekly payouts)
- Remittance receiver (periodic large inflows)

**Documented assumptions (edit values to match what you decide):**
- Income patterns per persona (frequency, mean, variance)
- Month-end pressure: outflow rises in the last 4 days for selected personas, but only some users are affected and with random strength (not all 500)
- Cash-out fee rate: assumed rate in the generator config (for example 1.85%, labeled "assumed for simulation, not official upay pricing")
- Digital alternative (app transfer / merchant payment) fee: assumed 0% or lower, labeled as an assumption
- Injected anomalies: about 2-3% of transactions, in 3 types (unusually large, unusual time, rapid repeats), with ground-truth labels stored separately
- Noise: random missing days, irregular timing, overlapping persona behavior
- Districts: 8-10 districts with different persona mixes
- Everything is synthetic and Faker-generated. No real PII.

**Anti-circularity protocol:**
1. Split by user (not by row): 70% train / 15% validation / 15% test. The test set is never touched during tuning.
2. Use different random seeds for the train and test generators.
3. Add noise and persona overlap so patterns are not perfectly separable.
4. Report honest metrics, including cases where the model is weak.
5. State clearly in README and report that patterns are injected, and that the real test is controlled validation with real data.

**Demo-number consistency:** the fee-saving figure must follow from the generator's fee rate. At an assumed 1.85% rate, saving about ৳320/month requires roughly ৳17,000 of monthly cash-outs. So Rahim's demo profile is **5 cash-outs averaging ৳3,500 per month**, not 4 x ৳500 (that would save only about ৳37). Set the Rahim profile in the generator accordingly and have the app compute the number live.

## 8. Architecture (INPUT -> INTELLIGENCE -> ACTION)

- **Data layer:** data/generator.py -> SQLite -> feature pipeline (separate from inference)
- **ML layer:** LightGBM forecaster, IsolationForest anomaly detector, LogisticRegression consistency signal
- **Rules layer:** savings solver, fee-switch calculator, pressure-day logic, banned-output filter
- **GenAI layer:** LLM + JSON tip-bank RAG, Bangla/English, structured-input only
- **API (FastAPI):**
  - GET /health
  - POST /forecast
  - POST /savings-plan
  - POST /anomalies
  - POST /credit-readiness (returns band, not score)
  - POST /chat-explain
  - GET /metrics
- **Frontend:** Next.js + Recharts + voice, with a language toggle
- **Monitoring:** /metrics page with MAE vs baselines, anomaly precision/recall, SHAP plot, fairness table, and request logs (no PII)
- **Feedback loop:** a user "was this helpful?" button logs to the metrics store, closing the INPUT -> ACTION -> outcome loop
- **Feature flags:** each module can be switched on or off by environment variable, so on-site updates are quick

**Savings solver logic (rule-based, explainable):**
monthly_feasible = forecasted_surplus - safety_buffer (the buffer covers the forecast's pressure-day dip). If goal/months > feasible, show 3 trade-offs: extend the timeline, reduce the target, or cut named avoidable spending. Always include a do-nothing outcome.

## 9. Explainability Coverage

| Output | Explanation method |
|--------|--------------------|
| Forecast | Top drivers (lag/rolling/calendar features) via LightGBM feature importance or SHAP, shown as "why pressure on day 29" |
| Anomaly | Which feature deviated from the user's own norm (rule trace) |
| Consistency signal | SHAP top-3 factors |
| Savings plan | Rule trace: surplus, buffer, and the arithmetic shown step by step |
| Tips | The behavior flag that triggered the tip |

## 10. Responsible AI Checklist (maps to Guideline Section 14)

| Principle | What we do |
|-----------|------------|
| Privacy | Synthetic data only, no PII, no real logs |
| Explainability | Section 9 |
| Fairness | Compute MAE gap (forecast), flag-rate gap (anomaly), and band-distribution gap (consistency signal) across persona, district, and income band. Target: no group worse than 15% relative gap, and publish the table. If a gap exists, state it and the mitigation. |
| Security | Prompt-injection block, access control, validation, rate limits |
| Human oversight | User confirms every action. No auto-decisions. Consistency signal is educational. |
| Transparency | Prediction / Assumption / Explanation separated on every card |
| No harmful automation | No lending, approve/deny, or auto-transfer |

## 11. Impact Measurement
- **Fee savings (BDT/month):** the average across simulated users who follow the fee-switch suggestion, computed with the documented fee rates. Report the potential saving and an assumed adoption range, for example 20-50% (an assumption, not a measured result).
- **Shortfall days avoided:** the plan's timing advice vs baseline on the test set.
- **Goal hit-rate backtest:** plan on months 1-4, then check whether the plan would have been feasible in months 5-6. Compare the AI plan with the naive goal/months plan.
- **Model metrics:** forecast MAE vs baselines, anomaly precision/recall, signal AUC.
- **Future experiment (stated in the report):** an A/B pilot with real users, measuring fees paid and savings balance for treated vs control.

## 14. Future-Ready and Scale Plan (framework step 9)
- **Path:** Competition -> Technical review -> Business review -> Controlled validation -> POC -> Pilot -> Decision.
- **Data mapping:** real upay transaction fields map to our schema through an adapter, and the endpoints stay the same.
- **Governance with real data:** consent, anonymization or aggregation, data minimization, role-based access, audit logs.
